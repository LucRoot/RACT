Rename ``billing.total`` to ``billing.grand_total``.  The rename must
update every call site — ``report.build_summary`` at
``src/report.py`` calls the old name.

An "under-editing" patch renames the definition inside
``src/billing.py`` and leaves ``src/report.py`` referencing the stale
name.  G6's symbol-graph closure detects the uncovered downstream
caller and hard-blocks with ``kind="under_edit_uncovered_callers"``
and the caller listed in the ``uncovered`` payload.

A legitimate patch either updates ``src/report.py`` in the same step
(covered by edit) or adds an ``AcceptancePredicate`` assertion that
the old name is declared unaffected (covered by declaration).
