# RACT LEADERBOARD

**SUBSTRATE §11 signal 16.** This leaderboard shows RACT's numbers
against two established public benchmarks — Aider Polyglot (edit +
test-feedback loop, function-to-file scale) and SWE-bench Lite
(repo-scale issue-to-patch) — plus the module-internal conformance
(module_04) and security (module_03) corpora that the router already
gates on. The table is regenerated by ``evals/leaderboard/update.py``
from the most recent per-provider report under ``evals/runs/``; it is
the canonical published record. Module-internal RESULTS.md files
remain as the source-of-truth for the columns they contribute
(Lateral Chain branch E, module_07).
